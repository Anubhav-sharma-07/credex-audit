## Infrastructure Costs at Scale

### 1,000 audits/month
- Groq API: $0 (well within free tier)
- Supabase: $0 (free tier = 50,000 rows)
- Vercel: $0 (free tier = 100GB bandwidth)
- **Total: $0/month**

### 10,000 audits/month
- Groq API: ~$5/month
- Supabase: $25/month (Pro plan)
- Vercel: $20/month (Pro plan)
- **Total: ~$50/month**

### 100,000 audits/month
- Groq API: ~$50/month
- Supabase: $25/month
- Vercel: $20/month
- **Total: ~$95/month**

## Why This Business Model Works
- Zero marginal cost per free user
- High margin on paid plans (~95%)
- Viral growth through shareable URLs
- Natural upgrade path (free → pro → enterprise)
- Low churn — teams re-audit every quarter
