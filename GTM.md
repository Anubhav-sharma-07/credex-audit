# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target Customer
Small to mid-sized tech teams (5–50 people) who subscribe to multiple AI tools and have no visibility into whether they're overspending.

## Problem
Teams are paying for overlapping AI tools, wrong plan tiers, and unused seats. The average team wastes $200–500/month on redundant AI subscriptions.

## Solution
A free 2-minute audit that shows exactly where the waste is and what to do about it.

## Distribution Channels

### Primary (Free)
- **Reddit** — Post in r/artificial, r/ChatGPT, r/cursor, r/SideProject
- **Twitter/X** — Share audit results as screenshots (viral loop)
- **Hacker News** — Show HN post
- **LinkedIn** — Target CTOs, Engineering Managers, Finance leads

### Secondary
- **Product Hunt** — Launch for visibility and backlinks
- **Dev.to / Hashnode** — Write "We audited our AI spend and saved $X" articles
- **Newsletter swaps** — Partner with developer newsletters

## Viral Loop
1. User runs audit → gets shareable URL
2. Shares URL with team or on social media
3. Team members click link → see results → run their own audit
4. Each share brings 2–3 new users

## Launch Sequence
- Week 1: Soft launch on Twitter/X and Reddit
- Week 2: Product Hunt launch
- Week 3: Hacker News Show HN
- Week 4: Analyze data, double down on what's working

## Success Metrics
- 100 audits in first week
- 30% of users share their results
- 10% email capture rate
- < 2 minute average completion time

## Pricing Strategy
- Free tier: unlimited audits
- Pro tier ($9/month): saved audit history, team comparison, CSV export
- Enterprise: custom pricing for large teams
