## FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. The audit is completely free, no credit card required.

**What tools do you support?**
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf.

**How accurate are the recommendations?**
Recommendations are based on current published pricing and typical usage patterns for your team size and use case.

**Do you store my data?**
Your audit results are saved so you can share them via URL. We don't sell your data.

**Can I share my results?**
Yes — every audit gets a unique shareable URL you can send to your team or manager.
